Privacy policy

Beanfolio · Updated: November 15, 2026

Your journal is stored locally

Beanfolio is a personal coffee and brewing journal. The app stores your coffees, brews, ratings, notes, photos, grinders and preferences locally on your device. Insights are also calculated on your device.

Beanfolio does not operate a server for your journal. There is no user account, automatic synchronization or transmission of your journal data to us. The installed app works offline.

No advertising or tracking

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking services. Beanfolio does not build usage profiles or send usage statistics or crash reports to us. Technical messages may appear locally in the device or browser console.

Camera, photos and files

When you want to add a photo to a coffee, you can use the camera or select an image. Beanfolio processes the selected photo locally and saves a copy for your coffee. The app does not upload your photos to a Beanfolio server.

Camera and photo access serve these actions that you initiate. You can change permissions in device settings and use the app without coffee photos. To import a backup, select the file in the file picker. A photo or file provider may download a file from its cloud storage at your request.

Exports and device backups

A backup you create contains coffees, brews, photos and your grinder list. Beanfolio does not encrypt the JSON file. You choose where to save it or which app or person to share it with. The selected recipients and storage services have their own privacy terms.

Depending on your operating system and device settings, app data may also be included in device backups or transferred when changing devices. This is not cloud synchronization operated by Beanfolio. The settings and privacy terms of your device or backup provider apply.

Retention and deletion

Your entries remain stored locally until you delete them or remove the app data. You can delete individual brews and coffees in the app. On Android, you can remove app data in system settings; in a browser, you can clear site data. On iOS, deleting the app removes local app data; offloading the app keeps it.

Deleting data in Beanfolio does not remove previously exported files, recipients' copies or device backups. Delete those copies separately at their storage location or provider. We cannot access your locally stored journal, so we cannot restore it or delete it remotely for you.

Protecting your data

The app uses the operating system's local app storage; the browser version uses your browser profile's storage. Protection also depends on your device lock, operating system settings and handling of exported files. Beanfolio does not provide an additional password lock for the journal or its exports.

Changes

If new features change how data is handled, we will update this policy and the privacy declarations in the stores accordingly.

Hosting of this website

This public privacy policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub receives technical connection data and logs IP addresses for security purposes, even without a GitHub account. The website includes no analytics or advertising services added by us. Reading the offline policy in the app does not load this website or transmit your journal to GitHub.

Further information: GitHub Privacy Statement.

Contact Beanfolio

For questions about privacy in Beanfolio, contact us by email. We use your message and email address to respond to your request. Our email provider processes this communication; contacting us does not automatically attach your journal.
